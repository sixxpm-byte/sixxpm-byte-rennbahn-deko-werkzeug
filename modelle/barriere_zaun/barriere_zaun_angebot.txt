• Endpfosten für Anfang und Ende einer Reihe

--- English ---
Just like at a real race track: concrete barriers with a tall catch fence separate the track from the spectators. This kit recreates exactly that – as a row you can make as long as your track needs. In 1:43 (Carrera GO!!!, Sturmkind DR!FT, slot cars) and 1:87 (H0).

Features
• Concrete element with race track profile, forklift pockets and slots for the post feet
• Double post with forked head standing on the joint: its foot pins reach into both neighbouring elements and so connect the barrier
• End posts for the start and end of a row
• Fence panels with diamond mesh and three tension cables, pushed into the posts with pins from the track side
• Fully plugged, no glue – join as many elements as you like
• All parts print without supports

Size
• 1:43: element approx. 9.3 × 1.5 × 2.1 cm, fence approx. 6 cm above the top, total height approx. 8.1 cm; row of 4 elements approx. 37 cm
• 1:87: element approx. 4.6 × 0.8 × 1.0 cm, fence approx. 3 cm above the top, total height approx. 4 cm; row of 4 elements approx. 18.5 cm

Included: STL and coloured 3MF files (barrier, post, end post, fence panel) in 1:43 and 1:87, colour lists with quantities for a row of 4 elements, readme and renders. The OpenSCAD source file is not included.
