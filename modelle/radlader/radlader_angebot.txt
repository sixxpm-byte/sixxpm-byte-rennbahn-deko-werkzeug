--- English ---
Bulk material for your race track or model railway: this wheel loader steers, lifts and dumps – in 1:43 (Carrera GO!!!, Sturmkind DR!FT, slot cars) and 1:87 (H0). You do not get a finished loader but a 3D-printable plug-together kit: you print the parts yourself and assemble them without glue using the illustrated printing and building instructions (PDF), the colour lists and the readme.

Features
• Articulated steering ±35° around a plug-in pivot pin
• Four wheels with lug tread and plug-in rims turn on square axles
• Lift frame with two lift arms raises the bucket from the ground to above the cab
• Bucket with Z-bar linkage like the real thing: tilt cylinder, tilt lever and link – roll back and dump at full height
• Three telescopic hydraulic cylinders: the silver piston rods extend with the movement and hold any position
• Rear frame with counterweight, rearward-sloping engine hood, radiator grille, exhaust, fenders, ladders and handrails
• Cab with plug-in glass core, mirrors and work lights
• Front fenders with lights, bucket with eight teeth
• Fully plugged, no glue; all parts print without supports and in one piece
• All colours are separate plug-in parts – printable part by part in single colours without an AMS

Size
• 1:43: 21.2 cm long, bucket 8.0 cm wide, cab 8.7 cm high
• 1:87: 10.5 cm long, bucket 4.0 cm wide, cab 4.3 cm high

Included: STL and multicolour 3MF files of all parts in 1:43 and 1:87, colour lists, readme, renders and two illustrated PDF instructions (printing and assembly). The OpenSCAD source file is not included.
