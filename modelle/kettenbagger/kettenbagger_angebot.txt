--- English ---
A construction site for your race track or model railway: this crawler excavator digs, swings and loads – in 1:43 (Carrera GO!!!, Sturmkind DR!FT, slot cars) and 1:87 (H0). You do not get a finished excavator but a 3D-printable plug-together kit: you print the parts yourself and assemble them without glue using the illustrated printing and building instructions (PDF), the colour lists and the readme.

Features
• Upper structure rotates all the way round on the undercarriage
• Boom, stick and bucket pivot on pins made from 1.75 mm filament
• Four telescopic hydraulic cylinders (two boom cylinders, stick and bucket cylinder): the silver piston rods extend with the movement and hold any position
• Bucket linkage like the real thing with idler links and connecting links
• Crawler tracks with grousers, rollers, idler and sprocket
• Upper structure with engine housing, counterweight, vent grilles, tank box, exhaust, handrails and mirrors
• Cab with plug-in glass core
• Bucket with teeth and side cutters
• Fully plugged, no glue; all parts print without supports and in one piece
• All colour parts plug together – printable part by part in single colours without an AMS

Size
• 1:43: undercarriage 12.5 × 7.8 cm, cab 7.4 cm high, boom 17.4 cm long
• 1:87: undercarriage 6.2 × 3.8 cm, cab 3.7 cm high, boom 8.6 cm long

Included: STL and multicolour 3MF files of all parts in 1:43 and 1:87, colour lists, readme, renders and two illustrated PDF instructions (printing and assembly). The OpenSCAD source file is not included.
