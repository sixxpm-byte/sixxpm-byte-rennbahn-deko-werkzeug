Features

• Full cab with rearward-leaning A-pillars, thick forward-leaning roll-over pillars with round cut-outs, door with cross bar, triangular side window, roof with work lights and louvred visor
• Glazing core in transparent filament, with driver's seat, steering wheel and dashboard underneath
• Tilt cylinders on the cab roof like the real machine – they also hold the mast at the top

• Fork carriage pluggable in four lift positions, forks slide sideways, wheels turn

• 3 colours + transparent; all colour parts plug together – printable part by part without an AMS

--- English ---
• Printer: FDM with 0.4 mm nozzle and a print bed of at least 220 × 220 mm; PLA or PETG, transparent filament for the glazing core
• Layer height 0.16–0.2 mm, 15 % infill, 3 perimeters, keep the slicer's bridging settings enabled
• Print every part exactly as oriented in the files – the orientation is ready to print
• Supports (on build plate only) for two parts: chassis (rear wheel arches, light housings and coupling recess at the rear) and cab (printed upside down: roof overhang, louvred visor, slanted roll-over pillars). All other parts print without supports
• Multicolour: print from the 3MF with AMS/MMU – or each STL on its own in one colour, the colour parts plug together; only the colour zones on the chassis and the mud flap on the fender need an AMS, otherwise print the 3MF in one colour
• Nothing is split – no clips needed; all parts fit on the bed in all three scales
• Fit in the Customizer: einsatz_spiel (rims, glazing core, cab; default 0.05 mm) and spiel (pins)
• Print time (slicer estimate at 100 % infill, one of each part): approx. 39 h for 1:24, approx. 9 h for 1:43, approx. 1.8 h for 1:87 – much less with 15 % infill
