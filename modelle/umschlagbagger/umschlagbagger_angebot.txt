Scrap yard, quayside or timber yard next to the track: this wheeled material handler (24-tonne class with twin tyres and four outriggers) is a 3D-printable plug-together kit – for Carrera GO!!!, Carrera Hybrid, Sturmkind DR!FT and slot cars in 1:43 and for the model railway in 1:87 (H0). You get print files, illustrated printing and building instructions as PDF, colour lists and a readme – not a finished model.

Features
• Chassis with axle housings, four plug-in outrigger pads, twin wheels with plug-in rims and a rotating upper structure
• Upper structure with engine house, rounded counterweight, tank box, vent grilles, exhaust and handrails
• Elevating cab like the real machine: parallelogram of four lift arms, a filament locking pin holds the cab in four heights
• Straight boom and drooped stick, each from two halves with dowels – no supports needed
• Two lift cylinders and one stick cylinder as telescopes: the piston rods hold every position by friction
• Five-tine orange-peel grapple with swing eye and rotator – the tines plug into the head from below
• All colour parts plug together (rims, glazing, piston rods, tines, handrails) – printable part by part in single colours without an AMS, no colour zones
• No glue: every joint is a pin cut from 1.75 mm filament

Size
• 1:43: chassis approx. 12 × 6 cm, cab roof 10–14 cm high, reach up to approx. 24 cm
• 1:87: chassis approx. 6 × 3 cm, cab roof 5–7 cm high, reach up to approx. 12 cm

Included: STL and multicolour 3MF files for 1:43 and 1:87, colour lists, readme, pictures and two illustrated PDF instructions (printing and assembly). The OpenSCAD source file is not included.

• Printer: FDM with 0.4 mm nozzle and a print bed of at least 220 × 220 mm; PLA or PETG
• Layer height 0.16–0.2 mm, 15 % infill, 3 perimeters, keep the slicer's bridging settings enabled
• Print every part exactly as oriented in the files – the orientation is ready to print: chassis, upper structure and grapple head upright, cab and cab carrier upside down, halves, lift arms, tines and handrails flat, cylinders and axles lying, tyres on their inner side
• No support material – every part is designed support-free (slicing check: 0 notes in both scales)
• Multicolour: print from the 3MF with AMS/MMU – or each STL on its own in one colour; all colour parts plug together, there are no colour zones
• Nothing split, no clips: boom and stick consist of two halves pressed together with dowels
• Cut 12 pieces of 1.75 mm filament as pins for the joints (lengths in the instructions and readme)
• Fit in the Customizer: zylinder_spiel (default 0.1 mm – smaller if boom or stick do not hold), einsatz_spiel (default 0.05 mm, press fit of rims, pegs, dowels and glazing)
• Print time (slicer estimate at 100 % infill): approx. 25 h for 1:43, approx. 4.5 h for 1:87 – much less with 15 % infill
